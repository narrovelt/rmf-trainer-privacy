RMF Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Summary

RMF Trainer does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app works entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics libraries.

Data stored on your device

To remember your study progress (tasks marked as studied and quiz best scores), the app stores a small amount of data in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with the "Reset progress" button on the home screen, by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Network access

The app's content is bundled with the app and works offline. It does not send your data to any server.

Children

The app is intended for adult learners and professionals. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Educational purpose and no affiliation

RMF Trainer is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NIST, the U.S. Department of Commerce, CISA, OMB or any other government agency. NIST publications referenced in the app are published by NIST and are the authoritative sources. The app does not provide legal, compliance or professional advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact details shown on the app's store listing.
